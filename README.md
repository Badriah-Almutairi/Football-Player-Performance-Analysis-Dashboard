⚽ Football Player Performance Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing football players’ performance using data analytics techniques.

The dataset was cleaned and prepared using Python and Pandas, then analyzed and visualized using Power BI to create an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard provides insights into player performance, market value, injuries, clubs, countries, competitions, age, and preferred foot.

⸻

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze football players’ performance.
* Compare players based on goals, assists, and clean sheets.
* Analyze market value across different playing positions.
* Explore injury statistics and total days missed.
* Analyze player distribution across positions, clubs, and countries.
* Explore player characteristics such as age and preferred foot.
* Build an interactive dashboard for sports analytics.
* Present meaningful insights through data visualization.

⸻

🛠 Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* SQL
* Microsoft Excel

⸻

📂 Data Preparation

The dataset was processed and prepared using Python and Pandas before being used in Power BI.

The data preparation process included:

* Loading and inspecting the dataset.
* Cleaning and organizing the data.
* Handling missing and inconsistent values.
* Removing duplicate records where necessary.
* Renaming and formatting columns.
* Combining relevant player-related data.
* Preparing cleaned datasets for analysis.
* Exporting the prepared data for Power BI.

⸻

📊 Dashboard Pages

🏠 Page 1 – Sports Performance Overview

The overview page provides a high-level summary of the football dataset.

Key Metrics

* Total Players
* Total Goals
* Total Assists
* Total Competitions
* Total Clubs
* Average Market Value

Visualizations

* Player by Position – shows the distribution of players across playing positions.
* Top 10 Countries – visualizes the geographical distribution of players.
* Top 10 Competitions by Goals – compares competitions based on total goals.
* Top 10 Clubs by Number of Players – shows clubs with the highest number of players.

⸻

⚽ Page 2 – Player Performance

This page focuses on individual and positional player performance.

Visualizations

* Average Goals by Position – compares the average number of goals across playing positions.
* Top Goalkeepers by Clean Sheets – highlights goalkeepers with the highest number of clean sheets.
* Top 10 Players by Assists – shows the players with the highest number of assists.
* Goals vs Assists – compares goals and assists across players.
* Top 10 Players by Goals – highlights the top goal-scoring players.

⸻

💰 Page 3 – Market Value & Injuries

This page focuses on player market value, injuries, age, and player characteristics.

Visualizations

* Total Injuries by Position – shows the distribution of injuries across playing positions.
* Average Total Injuries by Age – analyzes the relationship between player age and average injuries.
* Average Market Value by Position – compares the average market value across positions.
* Average Age by Position – compares the average age of players across positions.
* Total Days Missed by Position – shows the total number of days missed due to injuries by position.
* Players by Preferred Foot – shows the distribution of players based on their preferred foot.
* Top 10 Countries by Average Market Value – compares countries based on the average market value of their players.

⸻

📈 Key Insights

The dashboard provides insights into several aspects of football analytics, including:

* Differences in goal-scoring performance across playing positions.
* Players with the highest numbers of goals and assists.
* Goalkeepers with the highest number of clean sheets.
* Distribution of players across different positions and countries.
* Differences in market value between playing positions.
* Countries with higher average player market values.
* Injury distribution across different positions.
* Relationship between player age and average injuries.
* Total days missed due to injuries.
* Distribution of players based on preferred foot.

⸻

🔎 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to explore the data based on:

* Season
* Competition
* Country
* Team
* Position

The filters are available across the dashboard pages, allowing users to perform more focused and interactive analysis.
